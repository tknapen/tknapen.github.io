The first release of Arrow of Time, our intensive 7T fMRI dataset of brain responses to 2,160 brief naturalistic videos, is planned around the turn of the year. To be notified when it is available, fill in the [sign-up form](https://forms.gle/BMmfyWEZKL7vU5Va9).

Our [April post on the dataset](/2026/04/24/AOT.html) describes the stimuli and the scanning protocol.
