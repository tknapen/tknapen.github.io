The [Tiny Blue Dot Foundation](https://www.tinybluedotfoundation.org) is funding a new project in the lab on body-based meditation and psilocybin. [Xaver Funk](/people/xaver-funk/) will join the lab as a postdoctoral researcher in 2027 to work on it.

Related work from the lab includes the body maps we found in visual cortex ([Hedger et al., 2025](/publications/2025-11-26-NAT/)) and the effects of psilocybin on visual processing ([Aqil et al., 2025](/publications/2025-11-21-NC/)).
