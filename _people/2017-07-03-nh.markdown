Nick Hedger worked with the lab as a Leverhulme Trust fellow while holding a junior faculty position at the University of Reading. He worked on extending connective field models to other species and modalities, and was interested in clinical applications of these brain-based encoding models.

With Thomas Naselaris, Kendrick Kay and Tomas Knapen, he developed a model that maps somatosensory body-part tuning and visual field tuning across the brain at the same time. During movie watching, somatotopic tuning explained responses throughout dorsolateral visual cortex, revealing multiple body maps tiled across cortex and aligned with visual field tuning ([Hedger et al., 2025](/publications/2025-11-26-NAT/)).
