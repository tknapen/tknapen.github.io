Ron van de Klundert joined the lab as a PhD student in September 2021, after a master's in cognitive sciences at the University of Amsterdam. He started in the same month as Ningkai Wang. His PhD project was on brain-based encoding models: models that describe how responses in the brain depend on the sensory input, so that a model fitted to one set of measurements can be tested on its predictions for new stimuli.
