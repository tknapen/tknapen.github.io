Martin Szinte joined the lab in 2016 on a Marie Curie fellowship. He did his PhD in Patrick Cavanagh's lab in Paris, followed by an extended postdoctoral fellowship on a Humboldt grant in Heiner Deubel's lab in Munich. His expertise is in eye movements and their link to perception.

His Marie Curie project in Amsterdam set out to study eye movement planning and visual stability with 7T fMRI and MRI-derived forward models. With Tomas Knapen he showed that the default network deactivates as a function of the position of a visual stimulus, so that it encodes visual space by means of deactivation ([Szinte & Knapen, 2020](/publications/2020-05-18-CC/)).
