Tomas Knapen leads the lab. He has been a principal investigator and group leader in Cognitive Psychology at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam (Faculty of Behavioural and Movement Sciences) since 2013, and a senior researcher at the Spinoza Centre for Neuroimaging (KNAW) since 2017. Since 2019 he has also been affiliated with the Netherlands Institute for Neuroscience (KNAW).

He studied theoretical biology at the University of Amsterdam (MSc, 2002) and received his PhD in experimental physics from Utrecht University in 2007, in the "Physics of Man" group of the Helmholtz Institute. He did postdoctoral work at Université Paris Descartes, Vanderbilt University and the University of Amsterdam before starting the lab.

His research asks how the brain's topographic maps organize perception, action and social cognition. He came to neuroimaging from psychophysics, and the lab still puts weight on the behavioral side of every task. His training in a physics department shows in a preference for explicit models that explain the findings, and in the effort the lab spends on understanding the signals it measures as fully as possible.

His work has been funded by an NWO VIDI grant and by the Netherlands eScience Center, which supported the development of [prfpy](/software/) and, since 2025, supports Popylar, a community package for population receptive field modeling (Open eScience Call 2024).
