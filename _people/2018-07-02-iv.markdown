Inês Veríssimo was a PhD student in Chris Olivers' lab at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, co-supervised by Tomas Knapen. Her interests were in retinotopic and somatotopic mapping, and in the interactions between feature-based attention and visual-spatial representations.
