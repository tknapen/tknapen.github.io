Ningkai Wang is a PhD student in Jan Theeuwes' lab at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, co-supervised by Tomas Knapen. He started in September 2021 on a Chinese PhD scholarship. His project concerns statistical and reinforcement learning, and uses fMRI of the hippocampus to study them.
