Xaver Funk will join the lab shortly as a postdoctoral researcher, on a [project funded by the Tiny Blue Dot Foundation](/2026/11/10/tiny-blue-dot.html) that studies body-based meditation and psilocybin.

Xaver has a background in biology and cognitive science. He is currently affiliated with the Spinoza Centre for Neuroimaging at the Netherlands Institute for Neuroscience and with the PRSM lab at Leiden University, where he has run experimental studies at the intersection of perception, neuroimaging and psychedelics. He has led large-scale fMRI studies of vision and time perception under psilocybin, and of changes in resting-state network dynamics after psychedelic retreats. He is also interested in mathematical and computational modeling, open science and naturalistic study designs.
