Marco Aqil was a PhD student on Serge Dumoulin's VICI project, shared between the Dumoulin lab and ours. He developed new population receptive field (pRF) models to explain nonlinear processing in the visual system, and related these processing characteristics to their biological foundations.

He proposed a pRF model based on divisive normalization that captures disparate response signatures throughout the human visual hierarchy with a single computation ([Aqil et al., 2021](/publications/2021-11-15-PNAS/)). He then linked the model's parameters to serotonin and GABA receptor densities ([Aqil et al., 2024](/publications/2024-01-03-SA/)) and used it to study how psilocybin alters contextual computations in vision ([Aqil et al., 2025](/publications/2025-11-21-NC/)). With Tomas Knapen he developed [prfpy](https://github.com/VU-Cog-Sci/prfpy), the lab's package for fitting pRF models.
