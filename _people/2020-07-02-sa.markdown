Sumiya Sheikh Abdirashid was a PhD student on Serge Dumoulin's VICI project, shared between the Dumoulin lab and ours. Her project used population receptive field (pRF) models to study how attention influences visual processing.

Using 7T fMRI, she manipulated the precision of attention while measuring pRFs. Focused attention attracted pRFs toward the attended location more strongly than attention distributed across the display, and a Gaussian attention field model explained the results ([Sheikh Abdirashid et al., 2025](/publications/2025-09-02-JoV/)).
